Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Go Shop Me EOOD (“GoShopMe”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses and otherwise processes personal data in connection with the GoShopMe mobile application (the “App”), the GoShopMe web application, our AI shopping assistant ShAI (“ShAI”), our websites at goshopme.ai and goshopme.us (the “Websites”) and any related services we provide (collectively, the “Services”). It also describes the choices and rights available to you in respect of your personal data.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Services, you acknowledge that your personal data will be processed as described in it. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Services.

1. Who we are

Go Shop Me EOOD, a limited liability company registered in Bulgaria, is the controller responsible for the processing of your personal data in connection with the Services. We process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the “GDPR”), the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable data protection laws. Our contact details are set out in section 14.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal data that you provide to us directly, personal data generated through your use of the Services, and personal data collected automatically when you interact with the Services. If you choose not to provide certain information, you may not be able to use some or all of the features of the Services.

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account information, such as your email address, password, sign-in method and, where you choose to provide them, your phone number and date of birth. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form and are not accessible to us.
  • Profile information, such as your sizes, style preferences, budget, photographs, delivery addresses, birthday and notification preferences, which we use to personalise the Services.
  • Order and return information, such as the store, items, prices, order number, delivery details, tracking information and return deadlines, which you make available to us when you complete a purchase with ShAI’s assistance or forward an order confirmation to your GoShopMe address.
  • Communications, including feedback, support requests and any other information you choose to send to us.
  • Waitlist information, such as your name, email address, country and how you heard about us, when you register your interest on our Websites.

We do not collect or store your payment card details. Purchases are paid directly to the relevant store using the payment methods it offers.

2.2 Information collected through your use of the Services

When you use the Services, we process the content of your conversations with ShAI, including messages, photographs and links you share, as well as conversations in group chats you join. ShAI may derive preferences and contextual notes from your conversations (for example, your preferred fit or an upcoming occasion) in order to provide more relevant assistance in future conversations.

We also maintain a record of the actions ShAI performs at your request, the instructions and spending limits you set, and each approval you give, including your acceptance of our terms. Approval records are stored in a tamper-evident form, so that they may serve as reliable evidence of the actions you did and did not authorise.

The Services are not designed to collect special categories of personal data, such as information concerning your health, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, political opinions, racial or ethnic origin, or biometric or genetic data. If you share such information in a conversation, ShAI will use it only within that conversation, unless you explicitly ask ShAI to remember it, in which case we will process it on the basis of your explicit consent. You may withdraw that consent at any time by asking ShAI to forget the information.

2.3 Information we collect automatically

When you access or use the Services, we automatically collect certain information about your device and your interaction with the Services, including your IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, push notification token, error logs and records of visits to stores made through our affiliate links. We use this information to operate, maintain and secure the Services, and to diagnose and resolve technical issues. Further information about the technologies we use is provided in section 5.

We use personal data for the purposes described below, and only where we have a legal basis to do so under the GDPR:

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced those interests against your rights and freedoms. You may object to such processing as described in section 9.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to display advertising.

4. ShAI and automated processing

ShAI uses artificial intelligence models provided by third-party AI model providers acting on our behalf to understand your requests, search for products and assist you with purchases and returns. Your conversations and related information are disclosed to these providers solely for that purpose and under data processing agreements.

When ShAI searches for products, we disclose only the details of the product you are looking for, such as its description, size, colour and budget, together with your delivery country, to stores, product catalogue services and search services. We do not disclose your name, contact details or conversation history for this purpose.

Purchases and returns are carried out on your own device and in your own session with the relevant store. ShAI will not complete a purchase or submit a return without your explicit approval of that specific action or a standing instruction you have set within your own limits. The contract of sale is concluded between you and the store, and payment is made by you directly to the store.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our service providers use cookies and similar technologies, such as local device storage (“Cookies”), as described below.

The App. The App stores on your device only the information that is strictly necessary to provide the Services you request, such as your sign-in session, your settings and your notification registration. Such storage does not require your consent under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC (the “ePrivacy Directive”). The App does not use advertising or analytics Cookies and does not track you across other applications or websites. We will request your consent before introducing any Cookies that are not strictly necessary.

Store websites. When a store’s website is opened within the App, that store may set its own Cookies, which are governed by the store’s own cookie policy. These Cookies are stored on your device, separately from your device’s main browser.

Affiliate links. When you visit a store through one of our links, the store or its affiliate network may set a Cookie to record the referral, so that we may receive a commission. Such Cookies are governed by the policies of the relevant store or network.

Our Websites. Our Websites are hosted by Wix, which sets essential Cookies required for the Websites to function securely. With your consent, we also use functional Cookies to provide the chat window and remember your language preference. We do not currently use analytics or marketing Cookies on our Websites. You may accept or reject non-essential Cookies through our cookie banner and may change your choice at any time by selecting “Cookie settings”.

You can also manage Cookies through your browser settings. Please note that blocking essential Cookies may prevent the Websites from functioning properly.

6. Disclosure of your information

We may disclose personal data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Service providers that process personal data on our behalf and under our instructions, including providers of hosting and cloud infrastructure, database and authentication services, email delivery, website hosting, subscription payment processing and push notification services.
  • AI model providers, namely Google, Microsoft and Anthropic, which process personal data on our behalf to enable ShAI’s functionality.
  • Stores, product catalogue services and search services, which receive only the product details and delivery country described in section 4. Your relationship with a store is governed by that store’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Affiliate networks, which receive only a random identifier and not your personal data.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants and auditors, where necessary for the purposes of their engagement.
  • Successors, in connection with an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

We may also disclose personal data where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation or legal process, or is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property or safety of GoShopMe, our users or others.

7. International data transfers

The Services are hosted in the United States, and certain of our service providers process personal data in other countries outside the European Economic Area (“EEA”). Where we transfer personal data outside the EEA, we ensure that it is protected by appropriate safeguards, namely an adequacy decision of the European Commission, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, or the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission, together with supplementary measures where required. You may obtain further information about these safeguards, or a copy of them, by contacting us as set out in section 14.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to comply with our legal, accounting and reporting obligations, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. In particular:

Category of data Retention period
Account, profile, conversations, ShAI’s notes and activity records Until you delete your account
Waitlist information Until you unsubscribe or request deletion
Order and return records After account deletion, in a form that no longer identifies you, for as long as necessary to settle commissions and disputes
Approval records For the duration of your account and for ten (10) years following its deletion, solely for the purposes of disputes, chargebacks and complaints (Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR)

Backups Up to thirty (30) days, after which they are overwritten or deleted

9. Your rights

9.1 Rights under the GDPR

Subject to the conditions and exceptions set out in applicable law, you have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data and obtain a copy of it;
  • request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • request erasure of your personal data;
  • request restriction of the processing of your personal data;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have it transmitted to another controller; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

You may download your data and delete your account at any time in the App under Profile > Privacy Settings. Account deletion takes effect thirty (30) days after your request, during which period you may cancel it. Approval records are retained following deletion as set out in section 8. To exercise any other right, please contact us as set out in section 14. We will respond to your request within one month of receipt, which may be extended where permitted by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. Our supervisory authority is the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

9.2 Additional information for residents of the United States

If you reside in California or another U.S. state with a comprehensive consumer privacy law, you may have the right to know and access the personal information we collect, use and disclose; to correct inaccurate personal information; to delete your personal information; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and its use for targeted advertising; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined under applicable state law, and we do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

To submit a request, please use Profile > Privacy Settings in the App or email privacy@goshopme.ai with the subject line “Privacy request” and your state of residence. We will verify your request using information associated with your account and respond within forty-five (45) days. You may designate an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf, subject to verification of the agent’s authority. Our Websites do not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

10. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access, including encryption in transit and at rest and restricted access to production systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your personal data.

Because ShAI can take actions on store websites at your request, you should review the actions ShAI proposes before approving them, and keep your account credentials and device secure. If you believe that your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at support@goshopme.ai.

11. Third-party websites and links

The Services contain links to, and may open, websites and services operated by third parties, including stores and affiliate networks, that we do not own or control. Any personal data you provide to such third parties is governed by their own privacy policies and terms, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of such third parties, or for the content of their websites or services, and the inclusion of a link does not imply our endorsement of the third party or its content.

12. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended only for individuals aged eighteen (18) or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of eighteen (18). If we become aware that we have collected personal data from such an individual, we will delete it. If you believe that we may hold personal data relating to a minor, please contact us as set out in section 14.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by email at least thirty (30) days before any change takes effect. Where a change materially affects how we process your personal data, we will notify you at least two (2) months in advance and ask you to accept the change in the App before you continue to use the Services; you may delete your account free of charge before the change takes effect. The date at the top of this Privacy Policy indicates when it was last updated.
