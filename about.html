Our story

We’re building the personal shopper everyone deserves.

GoShopMe started with a simple frustration: shopping online takes too long, produces too many returns, and rarely feels personal. We built ShAI to fix that for every woman across the US and Europe, not just the ones who can afford a human stylist.

Nora Todorova Founder & CEO, GoShopMe
Meet the founder

Built by a woman who got tired of shopping the hard way.

I have always believed that great personal shopping should not be a luxury. Whether you have ten minutes or ten hours, whether your budget is €50 or €5,000, finding the right thing to wear should be effortless, not exhausting.

GoShopMe is my answer to years of late-night scrolling, wrong-size returns, and the creeping feeling that the internet has too many options and too little understanding of what I actually need.

ShAI is the personal shopper I always wanted. I am building her for every woman who has ever said “I have nothing to wear” and meant it.

— Nora Todorova

Our mission

“To make personal shopping accessible to every woman through AI that actually understands style, budget, and real life.”

We are not building another filter-heavy search engine. We are building a relationship between a shopper and an AI that learns, adapts, and genuinely helps from the first message to the last return.

What we stand for

Our values

Radically personal

Every recommendation ShAI makes is tailored to the individual. Her size, her taste, her budget, her life. Not the average shopper. Her.

Honesty first

ShAI tells you when something is out of budget, when an alternative is better value, and when something simply is not right for you. Honest advice over commission.

Time is everything

We build for women whose time is their most valuable asset. Every feature is designed to get you from needing something to wear to done, in minutes not hours.

Privacy by design

Your style profile, your size, your purchase history. All of it belongs to you. We use it to serve you better. We never sell it.

Built in public

We share our journey openly, the wins, the setbacks, and everything in between. The women who join our waitlist help shape GoShopMe before it launches.

Fashion without waste

By helping women buy the right thing the first time, we reduce unnecessary returns and impulse purchases. Better for your wardrobe, better for the planet.
