Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who we are

GoShopMe is an AI-powered personal shopping platform serving customers across the European Union and the United States. When this policy refers to GoShopMe, we, us, or our, it means the GoShopMe entity responsible for your data. For GDPR purposes, GoShopMe is the data controller.

You can contact us at any time at support@goshopme.ai.

2. What data we collect

We collect data you provide directly to us and data generated through your use of the platform.

Account and profile data includes your name, email address, and the style preferences, sizes, and budget information you share with ShAI. This data powers your personalised experience.

Transaction data includes information about purchases you make or initiate through GoShopMe, including order references, product details, and delivery information. For integration model purchases, payment data is processed by our payment service provider. We do not store your full card number or CVV at any point.

Usage data includes information about how you interact with GoShopMe, such as pages visited, features used, products viewed, and the contents of your ShAI conversations. We use this to improve recommendations and platform performance.

Device and technical data includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers. This is collected automatically when you access GoShopMe and is used for security, analytics, and platform optimisation.

Communications data includes emails, support requests, or feedback you send to us.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to provide, operate, and improve the GoShopMe platform and ShAI recommendations, to process transactions and facilitate returns, to send you transactional emails such as order confirmations and waitlist updates, to personalise your shopping experience based on your stated preferences, to analyse platform usage and improve performance, to detect and prevent fraud and security threats, and to comply with our legal obligations.

We will only send you marketing communications if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance when processing is necessary to provide the service you have requested, such as completing a purchase or managing your account.
  • Legitimate interests for analytics, fraud prevention, platform security, and improving our service, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent for marketing communications and non-essential cookies. You may withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation where we are required to process data to comply with applicable law.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as necessary to operate the platform.

Payment service providers process payment transactions on our behalf under strict PCI-DSS compliance. They receive only the information necessary to process your payment securely.

Merchant and affiliate partners may receive your order details, delivery address, and relevant product information when you make a purchase. For affiliate model purchases completed on a merchant website, that merchant receives and controls your data in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Technology providers who power our infrastructure include Supabase for authentication and database services, Wix for website hosting, Resend for email delivery, Google for analytics, and Vertex AI for AI model services. Each operates under data processing agreements with GoShopMe.

Legal and regulatory authorities where we are required by law to disclose information.

6. International data transfers

GoShopMe operates primarily within the European Union. Some of our service providers are based in the United States or process data in other countries. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

Google Cloud infrastructure including Vertex AI processes data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase operates primarily within the EEA. All international transfers are conducted in compliance with GDPR Chapter V requirements.

7. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes such as financial records which may be retained for up to seven years under applicable law.

Anonymised, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytics purposes.

8. Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United States, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access. You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification. You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure. You can request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction. You can ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability. You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@goshopme.ai. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In the European Union this is the relevant national data protection authority.

9. Rights for US residents

If you access GoShopMe from the United States, you may have additional rights under applicable state law.

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, the right to delete your personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and other US state residents may have similar rights under applicable state privacy laws. We will respond to verified requests from residents of any US state regardless of whether a specific law requires us to do so.

To submit a data rights request as a US resident, please email support@goshopme.ai with the subject line “Privacy Request” and the state you reside in. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

10. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and authentication requirements for all staff, regular security reviews, and industry-standard practices for our infrastructure providers. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at support@goshopme.ai.

11. Children

GoShopMe is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email or via the platform at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of GoShopMe after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact and complaints

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, please contact us at support@goshopme.ai. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. EU residents may contact the relevant national data protection authority in your country. UK residents may contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
